# v0.4.2: ready for a wider beta

Status: **being built** (owner, 2026-10-04). A patch release that fixes everything a pre-beta bug hunt found in [v0.4.1](releases/v0.4.1.md), before a larger beta.

## Scope (owner, 2026-10-04)

Four testers used v0.4.1 by role on a copy of the reference data (platform admin and auditor; a team owner and editor building an assistant; members, signed-out visitors, phones and keyboard; a visual sweep of every page at five widths) and filed 119 findings; the owner added two. After merging duplicates: 0 P0, 9 P1, 24 P2, about 70 P3. **All of them are in v0.4.2** (owner: nothing has been widely released yet). axe found no violations on any page tested.

The findings, with steps and screenshots, were kept with the test run; each is summarised in the milestone that fixes it below, under its tester ID (AD admin, BU builder, US users, VI visual, OW owner).

## Owner decisions

- **Scope:** every finding, P1 to P3 (2026-10-04).
- **OW-2, a Reranking page (option A):** reranking gets its own page, **Admin → Models → Reranking**, always in the sidebar like SystemOne: its status (on/off, model and health, candidates, time limit, agents that turned it off), a setup guide when nothing is set up (a connection that serves `/rerank` → a model of kind Rerank → choose it, with a button for each step and which are done), the settings (moved here from the Models page's dialog), a test with a sample query, and "Reranking: off · Set up" / "on · <model>" in Admin → Overview → Features. Before, its settings appeared only as a notice on Admin → Models once a rerank model existed, so nothing said reranking existed.

## As built: M1

Chat, the public page, the widget and conversations, with the answer pipeline's think-tag and suggestion fixes.

- **US-01 (= VI-02):** the root cause was the chat page's gate reading the shared `["conversation", id]` query's fetch state: while the chat loaded the conversation the gate showed "Loading the agent…", which unmounted the chat; the remount refetched the 404'd query and the conversations list, forever. The gate now waits only for its own lookup (a deleted agent's transcript). A 404 shows "This conversation isn't available." with **Start a new chat**, and a question typed there starts a new conversation. Test: one request to the unknown conversation, no loop (`chat-links.test.tsx`; it times out on the old code).
- **US-03:** sources are numbered by their first citation in the answer ("[2] … [1] … [4]" read 1, 2, 3), while streaming (markers are chips of the retrieved sources from the first words) and after, so no number changes at the end. Copy answer uses the same numbers and adds the source list; both exports renumber the text, the sources and the claims (`agents.ExportNumbers`), and the Markdown uses the reader's time zone (`tz`, additive). The stored numbers don't change (the viewer, claims and saved answers use them).
- **US-04, BU-10:** a suggestion must be a question (ends with a question mark, opens with ¿, or starts with a question word in one of seven languages) and mustn't just repeat a passage's title or heading; saved answers' suggestions are checked again on replay. The prompt also says not to reword the question. Try it was wired correctly: its suggestion call answered `NONE` (2 output tokens, usage event at 10:31:10, channel test), so nothing was shown.
- **US-05:** an unfinished marker ("[1") is hidden while streaming and dropped at Stop; a stopped answer says "Stopped. The answer may be incomplete." with **Ask again**, also after a reload. When Stop comes after the model finished but before the answer reached the reader (checks, moderation), the server stores it as stopped. Stop after `message_end` keeps the answer complete.
- **US-06:** decided: a `grounded_visit` cookie (random, HttpOnly, Path=/) is replaced at every sign-in and sign-out, and the anonymous cookie carries the visit it started in (`token.binding`); another visit's cookie is ignored. The anonymous cookies can't be cleared at sign-out because their path (`/v1/public`) keeps them from the auth endpoints. No migration. Sign in on the public page without SSO goes to `/?next=<page>` and comes back.
- **US-07:** relative dates count calendar days for 1–6 days ("2 days ago" for Friday evening on Sunday) in lists (`RelativeTime`), Home and ⌘K (`lib/relative-day.ts`). bitop-format's `formatRelativeTime` still rounds hours: a follow-up for M5/bitop-ui.
- **US-08:** the model's start is the `thinking` step (was `answering`), so the status reads "Thinking about your question…" then "Writing the answer…", in one wording on every page.
- **US-09:** the step's header is a grid (the icon stays beside a wrapping query), the answer starts clear of the steps, and a follow-up searched with the earlier question reads "“…?” with your earlier question". Not done: "Working on it…" before the first status (the wait for a model slot) would need a new status step in the API.
- **US-10:** the space before a chip doesn't break and the chip's punctuation sits right after it.
- **US-11:** pressing a pressed thumbs-up takes the rating back (`rating: none`, additive; the thumbs-down menu has **Remove my rating**); ratings no longer toast (the pressed thumb shows it; a status says it to screen readers). The toaster's position is the shell's (M5).
- **US-12:** a visitor's feedback refusal says "You're rating answers too quickly (limit: n per minute)" with `details.counter: feedback` (the limit is the questions' number, its counters are separate).
- **US-13:** "wifi" finds "Wi-Fi" (letters and digits compared, in SQL and for API keys); Discover draws its own chips row with the search as a chip and a **Clear all** that clears it (bitop-ui's FilterBar can't: a follow-up for M5).
- **US-14:** notification settings list team events only to people with the event's role in some team; the team Overview hides "Approved up to …" from members. Not done here: Connected apps' breadcrumb and repeated title are M5's VI-18; a profile page and an API keys entry in the account menu are shell features (M5/owner).
- **US-15:** renaming a conversation no longer changes its `updated_at`.
- **US-16:** the widget frame has one header (the loader's; the frame's name is for screen readers, and New chat shows in a slim row once there's a conversation), a compact welcome, scroll shadows on the conversation, the scroll button at the panel's end, a feedback menu that scrolls when taller than the panel, the cursor in the composer when the panel opens (the frame focuses it once loaded; not the editor's preview), and a refused site gets the refusal in place of the chat. The view staying at an answer's start is by design (a buffered answer is read from its start, `reveal-answer.tsx`). `/widget-demo` isn't a page: the demo is `make widget-demo` (docs/phase4-publishing.md).
- **US-17:** focus goes to the page heading after deleting a conversation; source cards draw their focus ring inside. Not done: the Rename dialog's `aria-modal`/inert background is bitop-ui's Dialog (M5); Escape outside the non-modal viewer is left as is.
- **US-18:** the conversations sheet has its own icon (not the navigation's), the chat header is slimmer in short windows (200% zoom), and the search step says "6 passages checked, 2 kept". The Conversations page's clipped segmented control is M5's VI-30.
- **BU-02:** reasoning in `content` is split out in the llm assembler (`internal/llm/thinktags.go`): `<think>…</think>` becomes thinking, and a bare `</think>` (Qwen3 with `enable_thinking: false` reasoning anyway after a tool result) turns the message's text so far into thinking, announced by a new event `text_to_thinking` that the answer pipeline uses to drop that text (kept as thinking). The repeat was that draft: the stored answer was "draft [3].\n</think>\n\nanswer [3]." in one turn. Partial tags are held across chunks. Tests: every chunking in `llm`, and the fake gateway's second turn after `search_knowledge`, streamed (signed in) and buffered (public).
- **VI-15b:** recent conversations in the sidebar and the chat's list show the whole title on hover (`title`; a bitop-ui tooltip on truncation is M5's).
- **VI-16:** the Conversations page and the chat's list show relative times with the exact time on hover.
- **VI-33:** the composer's keyboard hint is hidden on phone widths too (40rem); the ⌘K footer is bitop-ui's (M5).
- **BU-19 (from M2, the chat page, public page and widget):** measured with a new e2e check (`e2e/support/layout.ts`: every clipping ancestor and the window leave the composer's ring room; it catches Try it's clipping on the base commit) at six sizes, with the source viewer beside and under the conversation, on the public page and in the widget: the sides and bottom weren't cut off there. A screenshot showed the composer's top ring painted under the conversation (its scroll shadows, added above): the composer is now positioned, so its ring paints over them.
- **BU-20 (from M3, the widget items):** the title twice is US-16's one header; the scroll button sits at the panel's end instead of over the steps' text; a buffered answer whose question and steps fill the small window is shown from its text's start instead of its question (`reveal-answer.tsx`). The other BU-20 items (agents list version time, evaluation Compare labels and picker, KB Overview's Attach source) belong to M2/M3.
