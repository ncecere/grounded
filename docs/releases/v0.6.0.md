# Grounded v0.6.0

v0.6.0 is "service desk building blocks": five pieces that make Grounded easier to run as the front of a help desk. **Webhooks** tell other systems what happens; **agent templates** give a new agent a good start; a **router agent** sends each question to the right team's agent; **conversation sharing** lets someone show a colleague an answer; and **in-app team requests** let people ask for a team without leaving the app. Webhooks and team requests are **off until a platform admin turns them on**, and nothing else changes for an install that uses none of them.

Each item was built and checked on its own (tests with mutants, the CI suites on the homelab cluster, a QA walk of every page, and a manual walk clicking every control), and the walks found real bugs that are fixed here (see each item). Design notes: [`specs/v0.6.0/DESIGN.md`](../specs/v0.6.0/DESIGN.md); decisions: [`v0.6.0.md`](../v0.6.0.md).

## Highlights

- **Webhooks.** Another system gets a **signed** HTTPS `POST` with a small JSON event, **retried** when the receiver is down. Events: a web source synced or failed, an agent was published, an answer was rated (team webhooks), and a team was created or requested (platform webhooks). They carry **IDs and summaries, never transcripts or document text**. A platform admin turns them on (**Admin → Settings → Features → Webhooks**) and approves destination hosts (**Admin → Webhooks**); team admins and owners make a team's webhooks (**Team settings → Webhooks**). Addresses are `https` on port 443 to an approved host, checked when saved and before every delivery, with the crawler's guard (private, loopback, link-local and cloud-metadata addresses are never reached). Each webhook has a secret shown once and kept encrypted; deliveries are recorded in the transaction that made the change, retried over about a day, and a webhook pauses (and its admins are told) when the receiver says it is gone or five deliveries in a row give up. Reference: [`webhooks.md`](../webhooks.md).
- **Router agents.** A **router** answers nothing itself: each question goes to the best agent the person asking could chat with themselves (an agent opts in under **Share → Routers**), and the answer shows **which agent answered, with a link to carry on there**. It can never show anyone an answer they could not have asked for. When nothing fits it says so, and the question counts in its gap report. It needs the SystemOne scope check; the **Front door (router)** template starts one.
- **Agent templates.** **New agent → Start from** offers six built-in templates (service desk FAQ, policy explainer, course helper, public website assistant, internal knowledge assistant, front door) and any a platform admin saved (**Save as template…** on an agent; **Admin → Agent templates**). A template is how an agent answers, never what it answers from or who may use it: no knowledge bases, tools or audience.
- **Conversation sharing.** **Share…** in a conversation's menu makes a link to a **read-only snapshot** of your own conversation (questions, answers and their citations) for the people who can chat with the agent; an open link only for a public agent. The secret is shown once and revocable, and a share is a pointer, not a copy.
- **In-app team requests, optional.** Anyone signed in can ask for a team; platform admins review the queue (**Admin → Teams → Requests**), and approving **creates the team** with the requester as owner, optionally from a template. Off by default; an SSO group and a limit on open requests can narrow who may ask.
- **`TEST_TIME_SCALE` reaches the Go tests** (development only): the cluster's slower cores no longer fail waits that GitHub passes.

## Requirements

Unchanged from [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md#requirements). **Routers need the SystemOne scope check** (a SystemOne model with the scope check switched on); without it a router can't be published.

## Upgrading from v0.5.0

A rolling upgrade with no downtime. Take a backup as for any upgrade ([`operations/upgrades.md`](../operations/upgrades.md)), then change every `?ref=v0.5.0` in your overlay to `?ref=v0.6.0` and pin the new digests of `grounded` and `grounded-ocr` that the release's `*.digests.txt` list.

- **Five migrations** (the schema moves from version 48 to 53), all additive and quick: `00049` adds `agent_templates`; `00050` adds `team_request_settings` and `team_requests`; `00051` adds `conversation_shares`; `00052` adds `message_events.routing`; `00053` adds `webhook_settings`, `webhook_destinations`, `webhooks` and `webhook_deliveries`.
- **No new environment variables.** Webhooks and team requests are **off** after the upgrade; nothing is sent and no form appears until a platform admin turns them on. No agent is reachable through routers until its team opts in.
- **Key rotation** now also covers the webhooks' signing secrets (`webhooks.secret_cipher`): `grounded rotate-keys --dry-run` lists the column.
- **New notification types** appear in everyone's notification settings, on by default for the people they reach: `team.request_new` and `platform.webhook_paused` (platform admins only), `team.request_decided` (the requester) and `webhook.paused` (a team's admins and owners).
- **Before rolling back to v0.5.0,** switch routers off (unpublish or disable them): v0.5.0 does not know the `router` setting and would run a router as an ordinary agent with no knowledge bases, which only refuses. The new tables stay and are ignored; webhooks stop sending.
- **API (all additive):** templates (`GET /v1/agent-templates`, saving and managing them, `templateId` on agent creation); team requests (`/v1/team-requests…`, `/v1/admin/team-requests…`, `/v1/admin/settings/team-requests`); shares (`/v1/conversations/{id}/shares…`, `GET /v1/shared/{token}…`); routers (`routable` and `router` in an agent's configuration, `routedTo` on a conversation's messages and on the `message_end` event, `routable` and `router` on directory cards, the `no_agent` and `route_unavailable` reasons); webhooks (`/v1/teams/{team}/webhooks…`, `/v1/admin/webhooks…`, `/v1/webhook-events`, `/v1/webhook-destinations`, `/v1/admin/webhook-destinations…`, `/v1/admin/settings/webhooks`); `teamRequests` and `webhooks` in the `me` capabilities and the Features response.

## Known limitations

The [v0.5.0 limitations](v0.5.0.md#known-limitations) still apply, and:

- **Webhooks were not tested against a real external host.** Deliveries were exercised against a local TLS receiver; the address guard is unit-tested but not run against real DNS. Delivery is at least once and not ordered (receivers de-duplicate on the event ID); a secret's rotation has no overlap window; addresses are `https` on 443 only, with no custom headers or batching; platform webhooks do not receive teams' events.
- **Routers consider at most 25 reachable agents** (by name) and have no list of targets of their own; they cannot be tried in the editor or called with a service key (they answer in a person's or a visitor's conversation); a conversation holding routed answers cannot be shared. **A public (anonymous) router was not walked in a browser**; an integration test covers it.
- **Sharing has no expiry** beyond the conversation's own retention, and no view counts; a link is made and revoked, not edited.
- **Team requests cannot be edited** (withdraw and ask again), and cover a new team only, not a change to an existing one.
- **Templates:** the built-in ones are part of the release (a platform admin can't edit them); there are no team-level templates.
