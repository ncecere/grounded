# Grounded v0.4.0

v0.4.0 is about answers people can trust and teams can improve. Retrieval gets sharper with a cross-encoder reranker; a citation opens its passage in context; teams see the questions their agents couldn't answer, grouped into topics; repeated questions get a saved answer at once; and public answers stream paragraph by paragraph while every paragraph is checked. The design and the owner's decisions are in [`v0.4.0.md`](../v0.4.0.md); the full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).

A walkthrough by role (platform admin, auditor and accessibility, team owner and editors, public visitor and member) came before this release; its findings, including two privacy issues in the audit log and saved answers reused across conversations, were fixed before the tag.

## Highlights

- **Cross-encoder reranking** ([`operations/rerank.md`](../operations/rerank.md)). A platform admin chooses a rerank model (kind `rerank`, served at `POST {base}/rerank` in the Cohere/Jina shape by LiteLLM, vLLM, SGLang or Infinity) under **Admin → Models → Reranking settings**. Every search then reranks its fused candidates (40 by default) in one call and keeps the best few (6 by default, per agent in **Build → Advanced**), so SystemOne judges only those. It fails open: a slow or failed call keeps the previous order. Knowledge-base **Try it** shows rerank scores and can switch it off; evaluation runs can too, to compare.
- **The gap report** ([`gaps.md`](../gaps.md)). When an answer fails (nothing found, a strict refusal, every passage judged out, out of scope, an unsupported or contradicted claim, or a thumbs-down), its question is kept apart from the transcript and grouped with similar ones into **topics**, each labelled in a few words by the agent's chat model. **Gaps** (team sidebar; editors, admins and owners) lists them with counts and trends, and each topic offers **Add a source**, **Add to evaluations**, **Mark fixed**, **Dismiss for now** and **Not for this agent**. A topic shows only once **3 different people** asked, and never shows a question's text or who asked, unless the asker ticked **Share this question with the team** on a thumbs-down (off by default). Platform staff see counts per team only. [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-conversation-privacy-and-retention.md) is amended for this.
- **The source viewer** ([`source-viewer.md`](../source-viewer.md)). A citation's **Show source**, or a source card, opens the cited passage highlighted among its neighbours, beside the conversation (a full-screen sheet on phones and in the widget), with the claims that cite it and their verdicts, **Open the page** for web pages and **Open full document** for the team's editors. Source cards break down their claims: "Supports 3 claims · 1 not supported".
- **Saved answers** ([`answer-cache.md`](../answer-cache.md)). The first question of a conversation, asked again under the same conditions (agent version, knowledge, settings), gets the saved answer at once and at no model cost, with the same citations. Exact by default, near-identical questions optionally (confirmed by SystemOne). On by default for public agents, per agent in **Agent → Settings → Saved answers** (24 hours by default), a platform switch in Admin → Overview → Features. Invisible to people; analytics show the hit rate and tokens saved.
- **Answers streamed in checked paragraphs** ([`moderation-streaming.md`](../moderation-streaming.md)). A third moderation output mode: the answer is released paragraph by paragraph, each checked with everything before it; a failing paragraph replaces the whole answer with the notice. It's the new default for the Public audience (an admin's saved policy keeps its mode).
- **Reasoning you can turn down, or off.** A chat model's **How to turn thinking off** (Admin → Models → Compatibility: `reasoning_effort: none` or `enable_thinking: false`) lets agents choose **Off**; each audience's moderation policy has a reasoning effort, **Low** by default for Public. While a reasoning model thinks, people see "Thinking about your question…".
- **Feedback on the public page and in the widget.** Visitors rate answers like signed-in people, with the reasons and the share tick; a thumbs-down feeds the gap report and removes a saved answer.

## Requirements

Unchanged from [v0.2.0](v0.2.0.md#requirements). Reranking needs a rerank model on your gateway only if you turn it on.

## Upgrading from v0.3.1

A rolling upgrade with no downtime. Take a backup as for any upgrade ([`operations/upgrades.md`](../operations/upgrades.md)), then change every `?ref=v0.3.1` in your overlay to `?ref=v0.4.0` and pin the new digests of `ghcr.io/ncecere/grounded` and, if you run OCR, `ghcr.io/ncecere/grounded-ocr`. Refresh the dashboards and alert rules from `deploy/observability`.

**Migrations `00040` to `00044`** run on start (schema version 39 → 44). They are expand-only, so v0.3.1 pods keep working against the new schema while the rollout proceeds:

| Migration | What it adds |
|---|---|
| `00040_rerank` | The platform's rerank settings and the priced units of rerank requests |
| `00041_gap_report` | Failed questions and gap topics (new tables), and whether a thumbs-down shared its question |
| `00042_answer_cache` | Saved answers and their settings (new tables), a content revision on knowledge bases kept by triggers |
| `00043_gap_uncited` | Removes the `uncited` signal from questions captured by pre-release builds (an uncited sentence alone isn't a failure) |
| `00044_gap_topic_history` | The two kinds of dismissal and a topic's history, and the per-team gap settings |

**No new environment variables.**

Things people will notice:
- **Public answers** stream in checked paragraphs, unless an admin had saved the Public moderation policy (it keeps Buffer or Stream, then retract; switch it in **Admin → Safety → Moderation** to get the new mode). Public answers use **Low** reasoning effort for models that accept it.
- **Saved answers are on for public agents.** Repeated first questions are answered at once; a thumbs-down, a change to the agent or its knowledge, the expiry or **Clear saved answers** retires them.
- **The widget starts its own session**, tied to its publishable key, so the key's limits, switch and allowed origins always apply. Visitors who used the widget on the same site as the public page start one new conversation after the upgrade.
- **Gaps** appears in the team sidebar after Evaluations; topics take up to an hour to form (an hourly job).
- **Admin → Models** has **Reranking settings**; nothing is reranked until a rerank model is chosen.
- **Costs** has a **Rerank** category, and cost CSVs a `rerank` column after `mcp`.
- **The audit log**: `gap_topic.*`, `agent.answer_cache_update`, `platform.answer_cache` and rerank settings changes; evaluation questions' audit entries no longer carry their text.
- **Metrics:** new `grounded_rerank_requests_total`, `grounded_rerank_duration_seconds`, `grounded_gap_questions_total`, `grounded_gap_topic_changes_total`, `grounded_answer_cache_lookups_total`, `grounded_answer_cache_tokens_saved_total`, `grounded_answer_cache_entries`, `grounded_moderation_first_release_seconds` and `grounded_moderation_paragraph_checks_total`.
- **API** (additive): reranking settings and status, gap topics and their actions, gap settings, cited passages in context and whole-document text, public feedback, saved-answer settings; a new `status` step `thinking`, `mode` on `message_start`, `rerankScore` on retrieval results, `stream_checked` among moderation modes, `off` among reasoning efforts.

## Known limitations

The [v0.3.0 limitations](v0.3.0.md#known-limitations) still apply. For the new features:
- **Saved answers** cover only the first question of a conversation, and not agents that let the model decide when to search.
- **Gap topics** are formed by an hourly job; resolving a topic automatically ("answered well again") works for agents that search before every answer.
- **Stream checked paragraphs:** after a failing paragraph the model finishes in the background (so its usage is metered); JSON answers, the non-streamed OpenAI-compatible endpoint and MCP `ask` are checked whole.
- **The source viewer** serves only passages an answer cited; whole documents are for the team's editors, admins and owners.
