# v0.8.0: identity and compliance

Status: **being planned** (owner, 2026-10-04). Planned ahead with v0.6.0 and v0.7.0 so the next versions are mapped to the roadmap ([`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) § L). Decisions are recorded here as the owner makes them; the full design is written before building.

## Scope (owner, 2026-10-04: option A)

The questions an institution's security and compliance review asks before a wide rollout: who gets access, what's logged and where it goes, what sensitive data is indexed, what each agent may spend.

| # | Item | Roadmap |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SCIM provisioning**: users created and suspended from the identity provider, building on SSO groups | E4 |
| 2 | **Audit export and SIEM streaming**: signed daily exports; streaming to Splunk, Elastic or syslog | E5 |
| 3 | **PII scanning at ingest**: flag or block documents with national ID numbers, card numbers and similar | B7 |
| 4 | **Per-agent budgets** (the rest of the budget follow-ups) | E17 |
| 5 | **Audience in the usage ledger**: usage split by audience (public, signed-in, widget, API, MCP) | E13 |
| 6 | **Auditors see read-only facts** on admin team settings | E16 |

Not in v0.8.0: Microsoft Teams and Slack bots (D1; after v1.0.0, owner); crawling sites behind a login (B5; with v0.9.0's document permissions).
